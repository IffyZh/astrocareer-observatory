# Data Rights and Publication Policy

[简体中文](DATA_RIGHTS.zh-CN.md)

## Current status

The Apache License 2.0 in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) covers the project's original software, tests, configurations, database schema, and project-authored documentation unless a file states otherwise. It does **not** grant rights to third-party job advertisements, web pages, logos, trademarks, datasets, or other source material.

No general open-data license is currently granted for collected records or database exports. A future dataset release must state its license and exclusions explicitly. Public access to a source does not by itself mean that its contents may be copied or relicensed.

Exception: ROR IDs and registry metadata imported from official ROR data dumps retain their source's CC0 1.0 public-domain dedication. This does not apply to ROR logos, general website content, or the websites of organizations listed by ROR.

## Third-party material

Rights in source material remain with their respective owners. Source-specific terms, copyright, database rights, privacy rules, robots policies, and redistribution restrictions must be reviewed before automated collection or publication.

Unless review establishes that redistribution is permitted, public records should contain only the minimum supported structured facts needed for research, together with provenance such as the source URL, observation time, evidence location, and integrity hash. The project should not republish full advertisement text, images, or branding by default.

Facts, short extracts, and metadata can still be subject to jurisdiction-specific rules. Inclusion in this repository is not a representation that every downstream use is lawful in every jurisdiction.

## Provenance and uncertainty

Every published observation should preserve:

- its original source and observation time;
- whether a value was explicitly stated, inferred, calculated, or unavailable;
- the applicable source-access and redistribution review;
- corrections and material transformation history;
- any source-specific attribution or license notice.

Unknown, undisclosed, and not-applicable values must remain distinguishable. Inferred immigration difficulty, eligibility, selection rates, and similar analytical fields must not be presented as official source statements.

## Personal and sensitive data

The public dataset must not contain private applicant profiles, names of unsuccessful applicants, contact details collected for personal use, application histories, immigration documents, credentials, or protected personal characteristics. Aggregate application and hiring counts may be stored only when supported by a legitimate public source and when they do not enable re-identification.

Personal notes and individualized career models belong in a separate private layer that is excluded from version control and public releases.

## Future dataset licensing

Before selecting an open-data license, the project will complete source-by-source rights reviews and define exactly which original database rights, if any, it can license. Possible future licenses include CC BY 4.0 for attribution-based reuse or ODbL 1.0 for database share-alike. Choosing either license will not relicense excluded third-party content.

Questions about a record's rights or provenance should be treated as data-quality issues. When in doubt, withhold the affected content from public exports until it has been reviewed.
